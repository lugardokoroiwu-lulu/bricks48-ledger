          Rooms as at time of leaving

          Tap EMPTY to book a room (1 Tissue and 1 Soap are issued to it automatically) — you will be asked for the amount, which is recorded straight away (the room's last price is suggested). Or type a price directly — it marks itself BOOKED and defaults to Paid in full, cash. Change Pay, Method or Guest as needed; unpaid or partly-paid rooms show up under Receivables. Tissue, soap etc. given to a room are recorded under Supplies.
